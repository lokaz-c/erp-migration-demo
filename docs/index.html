It is not DIKAM Fashion's production code or data: every supplier, product, employee and amount here was generated (seed 42, books for 2015-2024), and every number on this page was computed by make demo, not typed.

Duplicates merged

Each domain has a natural key (PO number and product, receipt and product, voucher number, month and employee, SKU). Rows are ranked within each key with row_number() over (partition by key order by book, sheet, row); the first is kept. Later rows with identical values are merged into it. Later rows with different values are rejected as CONFLICTING_DUPLICATE, because two clerks disagreeing is a question for a person.

How the thresholds were chosen

The thresholds were calibrated on spellings generated from five other seeds (101, 202, 303, 404, 505), never on the seed this report scores. The auto-merge threshold is the one with the highest recall among those with pooled precision of at least 0.99; the review threshold is the lowest 5-point score band where at least half of the suggestions are right. Calibration picked 88 and 73; the matcher is configured with 88 and 73.

Dates

Books mix real Excel dates, serial numbers, ISO text, text months and slashed dates typed day-first or month-first. A slashed date with a part above 12 settles the convention for its sheet; ambiguous values take the sheet's convention, then the workbook's, then are resolved from neighbouring rows when a sheet mixes both (books are in date order), and are otherwise rejected as AMBIGUOUS_DATE. Dates outside the book's year (plus 62 days) are rejected as DATE_OUT_OF_RANGE.

Amounts and currencies

A currency column wins; otherwise a marker inside the amount text ("$1,250.00", "125,000 Frw"); otherwise the column header ("Amount (Frw)"). A currency column that contradicts the amount text is CURRENCY_CONFLICT; no currency at all is UNKNOWN_CURRENCY. USD amounts are converted with core.fx_rates, a synthetic table generated by a formula (1000 RWF per USD in January 2015, plus 5 per month). It is not market data.
